<objective>
A reviewer can claim an open item (one winner under concurrency), release their own claim, and expired proposals are lazily withdrawn with a distinct, committed 409 proposal_expired.

Purpose: the queue's concurrency and expiry guarantees are the heart of QUE-05, QUE-06, QUE-10 and POL-08. The same expiry-in-transaction helper is reused by the decide path in plan 09. Slice: reviewers claim and release over HTTP; time passing past expires_at turns the next claim or release into a committed withdrawal.
Output: store claim/release/expiry, service, two POST routes, reasons, tests.
</objective>
