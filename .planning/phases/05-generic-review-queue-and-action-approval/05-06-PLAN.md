<objective>
Let a supervisor-role reviewer see exactly what they are asked to decide (the proposal content, via the reviewer-scoped payload endpoint) and let any tenant reviewer read an item's append-only audit history.

Purpose: reviewers need the content to decide while the queue itself stays reference-only (QUE-01); the role gate that Phase 7 keeps is exercised for the first time here (D-19), and the history endpoint lets later plans prove claim, release, decision and withdrawal events (QUE-09) over HTTP.
Output: two new reviewer routes, service methods, DTOs and tests.
</objective>
