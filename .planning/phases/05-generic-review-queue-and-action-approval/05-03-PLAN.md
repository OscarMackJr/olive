<objective>
Make a policy-passing proposal appear as an open action_approval item, atomically with its persistence (QUE-03), and give the application a tenant-safe read side for the queue (list, get, events, reviewer payload).

Purpose: this is the enqueue seam every later reviewer action depends on. Atomicity and "refusals never enqueue" are provable here at the store/database level through the existing submit endpoint, before any reviewer authentication exists. Slice: submit a proposal over HTTP, then observe the item and its created event in the database and through IQueueStore.
Output: IQueueStore contract (read side now; later plans add claim/release/decide members), QueueSql, PostgresQueueStore read methods, the enqueue statements inside the accept transaction, tests.
</objective>
