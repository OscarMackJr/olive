<objective>
Vertical slice: an agent that submits an unknown action or a wrong-purpose proposal gets a stored, replayable refusal with a policy_evaluated audit event, and it never becomes queue-eligible. Fixes the CONCERNS.md "unknown action throws" defect.

Phase Goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): Policy decides before any human sees a proposal. Out-of-purpose, unentitled or rate-limited proposals are stored as refusals and never queued, every lifecycle transition is guarded, and the full submission order is enforced and asserted.

Purpose: POL-01, the refusal half of POL-04, and POL-05 for the new reasons.
Output: PolicyEvaluator (purpose and catalog checks), refusal events with detail, endpoint wiring, tests.
</objective>
