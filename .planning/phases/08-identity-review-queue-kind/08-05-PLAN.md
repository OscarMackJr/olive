<objective>
Reviewer side of the identity_review kind through the existing JSON API (IRQ-02, D-09, D-11, D-12). Vertical result: a steward claims a seeded or created identity item, defers it, re-claims it and accepts it; non-stewards are refused; history shows every step.

Decisions recorded: non-steward claim risk accepted (Phase 5 Q7 keeps claim role-free; a non-steward who claims an identity item can only release it, or bluto can withdraw it; RESEARCH Pitfall 9 and Open Question 2). Existing tests that break here and are fixed in this plan: `QueueServiceDecideTests.QUE_07_identity_review_item_is_kind_not_supported...` (identity is now supported; rewrite to keep an unknown-kind/unknown-decision `KindNotSupported`/`InvalidRequest` case), and the fake `IQueueStore` in the same file (new members).
</objective>
