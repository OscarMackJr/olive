## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 6 goal (not yet in user-story form; run `/gsd mvp-phase 6` if a story is wanted): An approved proposal executes exactly once in simulation, and the whole core-value chain can be queried from the execution record.

This plan is the Wave 1 shared foundation for the phase: the INSERT-only ledger and the chain view that every later slice writes to or reads from. It is observable on its own through the privilege-matrix, RLS and constraint tests.

Output: migration 0009, ExecutionLedgerTests, updated pin tests, execution-record v1 contract with valid/invalid examples.
